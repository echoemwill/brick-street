DASHBOARD GUIDE

How to use this dashboard

Every signal on this page is built from verified institutional data. Here is what each piece means and how professional investors act on it.

▲

Where do the analyst ratings come from?

All Buy, Hold, and Sell ratings are sourced from MarketBeat, aggregating official recommendations from licensed analysts at major investment banks. Each rating is a formal, published call from a credentialed professional — not an algorithm.

◈

What does a strong Buy consensus mean?

When 10 or more independent analysts converge on a Buy, it signals institutional conviction across multiple research desks. A strong consensus is one of the most reliable forward-looking signals available to retail investors.

◉

Stocks with strong ratings have growth potential

Stocks passing our filter represent names where Wall Street has the highest collective conviction. Research shows strong analyst consensus tends to outperform the broader market in the near to mid-term.

⏸

Why are Hold and Sell stocks filtered out?

High Hold counts signal analyst uncertainty — ambiguity that often precedes stagnation. Sell ratings carry explicit warnings. We filter both so every stock you see has a clean, directional signal behind it.

◎

Reading the Earnings Surprise column

The surprise % shows how much a company beat or missed analyst EPS estimates each quarter. Consistently positive surprises signal a company repeatedly outperforming Wall Street's best forecasts — a powerful quality indicator.

◆

Using the Fed Rate panel

The Federal Reserve rate is the most powerful lever in global markets. High rates compress valuations — especially tech. When the Fed cuts, equities expand. Use the left panel to track the current rate and next decision before positioning.

▣

Trading around the Economic Calendar

The largest S&P 500 moves are triggered by scheduled releases — CPI, NFP, GDP, FOMC. Professional traders adjust positions before these events. The right panel shows what is coming, consensus forecasts, and which sectors are most exposed.

⚠

Important — Not financial advice

This dashboard is a research and screening tool only. It does not constitute personal financial advice. Always conduct your own due diligence and consult a qualified financial advisor before making any investment decision.

INTELLIGENCE SOURCES

Where the data comes from

Every number on this dashboard is sourced from verified, institutional-grade data providers. No estimates, no fabrication — only authoritative data from the organisations that Wall Street itself relies on.

ANALYST RATINGS
MarketBeat Visit ↗

One of the most trusted financial intelligence platforms in the industry, used by both retail and institutional investors. MarketBeat aggregates official Buy, Hold, and Sell recommendations from licensed analysts at Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, JP Morgan, and 200+ other major investment houses — updated daily.

  • Official recommendations from licensed Wall Street analysts
  • Covers every stock in the S&P 500
  • Updated daily as new ratings are published
  • Tracks analyst consensus going back years
PRICE & EARNINGS
Yahoo Finance Visit ↗

Yahoo Finance is the world's most widely used financial data platform, serving over 75 million users. We use it to retrieve real-time stock prices and quarterly earnings history — specifically the EPS surprise percentage that reveals whether companies are consistently beating or missing Wall Street expectations.

  • Real-time and delayed stock price data
  • EPS actual vs estimate for last 4 quarters
  • Earnings surprise % calculated per quarter
  • No API key required — publicly accessible
FEDERAL RESERVE DATA
FRED — St. Louis Fed Visit ↗

The Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis publishes FRED — Federal Reserve Economic Data — one of the most authoritative economic databases in the world. We use it to retrieve the official Federal Funds Target Rate, giving you the most accurate and up-to-date picture of current US monetary policy directly from the source.

  • Published by a US Government institution — the Federal Reserve
  • Public domain data, free with no restrictions
  • Official rate history from 1954 to present
  • Updated on the day of each FOMC decision
ECONOMIC CALENDAR
Finnhub Visit ↗

Finnhub is an institutional-grade financial data provider trusted by hedge funds, fintech companies, and professional trading desks worldwide. We use their economic calendar API to surface upcoming high-impact US economic releases — the same events that move billions of dollars in the markets every month — complete with consensus forecasts and prior readings.

  • Institutional-grade data used by professional traders
  • Covers all major US economic releases (CPI, NFP, GDP, FOMC)
  • Consensus estimates from the analyst community
  • Real-time updates as events release
